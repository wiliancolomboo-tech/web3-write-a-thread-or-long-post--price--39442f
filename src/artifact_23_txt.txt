6/9 Taker Fee Realities on Solana:

• Orca Whirlpools: Dynamic fee tiers (1 bps, 2 bps, 4 bps, 16 bps, 100 bps) tuned to tick volatility.
• Raydium CPMM: Fixed 25 bps protocol fee (non-optimal for low-volatility stable pairs).
• Phoenix: Ultra-lean taker fee (~1-2 bps) with no liquidity provider divergence loss (no impermanent loss).

For high-volume pairs, Phoenix and Orca 1-4 bps tiers dominate effective pricing.
