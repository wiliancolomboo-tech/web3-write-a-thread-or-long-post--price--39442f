# Dissecting Solana Price Execution: A Quantitative Comparison Across Jupiter, Raydium, Orca, and Phoenix

Solana's sub-400ms slot times and continuous block building create the fastest DeFi trading environment in Web3. However, "best execution" on Solana is far more nuanced than simply picking the pool with the lowest headline fee tier. 

Between concentrated liquidity tick traversals, order book matching engines, priority fee auctions, and dynamic compute unit (CU) metering, calculating the *True Realized Price* requires an end-to-end audit of on-chain mechanics.

In this technical breakdown, we benchmark the four primary price discovery venues on Solana:
1. **Jupiter v6:** Graph-traversal dynamic routing & meta-aggregation.
2. **Orca Whirlpools:** Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) using logarithmic tick arrays.
3. **Raydium CLMM:** Concentrated liquidity optimized for Solana high-frequency rebalances.
4. **Phoenix:** Fully on-chain, non-custodial limit order book (CLOB) without off-chain crank dependencies.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of Net Price Execution ($P_{\text{eff}}$)

When executing a swap of size $Q$ from Token $A$ to Token $B$, the naive execution price is $P_{\text{headline}} = \frac{Q_B}{Q_A}$. 

On Solana, the **Net Effective Price** ($P_{\text{eff}}$) must account for transaction prioritization and account locking:

$$P_{\text{eff}} = \frac{Q_B - \Phi_{\text{DEX}} - \Phi_{\text{ATA}}}{\left( Q_A \cdot P_A \right) + C_{\text{compute}} \cdot \mu_{\text{price}} + T_{\text{Jito}}}$$

Where:
- $\Phi_{\text{DEX}}$ is the venue-specific pool protocol/LP fee (e.g., 1 bps to 100 bps).
- $\Phi_{\text{ATA}}$ is the idempotent Associated Token Account allocation fee (0.00203928 SOL rent-exempt, if not initialized).
- $C_{\text{compute}}$ is the exact Compute Units consumed (ranging from ~35k CU for Phoenix to ~350k CU for multi-hop Jupiter).
- $\mu_{\text{price}}$ is the priority fee rate in micro-lamports per Compute Unit (`ComputeBudgetInstruction::set_compute_unit_price`).
- $T_{\text{Jito}}$ is the MEV tip passed via direct tip-account transfer to prevent sandwich attacks in volatile blocks.

## 2. Venue-by-Venue Deep Dive

### A. Phoenix (Ellipsis Labs): The Zero-Crank On-Chain CLOB
Unlike legacy Solana orderbooks that relied on asynchronous cranks to match orders and clear events, Phoenix executes and settles state atomically within a single transaction instruction.
- **Price Mechanism:** Discrete price ticks and limit order queues.
- **Latency Profile:** Instant fills against rested resting limit orders; zero curve slippage within current spread.
- **Compute Unit Footprint:** **~35,000–55,000 CU** per market fill.
- **Best Suited For:** Large institutional block trades ($> $100k) where AMM tick slippage would exceed the orderbook spread.

### B. Orca Whirlpools: Concentrated Liquidity & Tick Arrays
Orca allocates liquidity within discrete tick ranges $[\sqrt{P_{\text{lower}}}, \sqrt{P_{\text{upper}}}]$.
- **Price Mechanism:** $L = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta \sqrt{P}} = \Delta x \frac{\sqrt{P_{\text{upper}}} \sqrt{P_{\text{lower}}}}{\sqrt{P_{\text{upper}}} - \sqrt{P_{\text{lower}}}}$
- **Tick Traversals:** When trade size exceeds the liquidity of an initialized tick array, the swap cross-references subsequent tick arrays. Each tick array load requires passing an additional account key, inflating transaction byte size and CU overhead.
- **Compute Unit Footprint:** **~60,000 CU** (single tick) to **~140,000 CU** (crossing 3+ tick arrays).
- **Best Suited For:** Medium-sized retail trades ($1k – $50k) across pegged (USDC/USDT) and high-correlation pairs (SOL/mSOL/bSOL).

### C. Raydium CLMM & CPMM
Raydium remains Solana's foundational liquidity layer, supporting both legacy Constant Product ($x \cdot y = k$) and Concentrated Liquidity.
- **Price Mechanism:** Raydium CLMM maintains price curves with customized fee tiers (0.01% up to 1%).
- **Execution Nuance:** Pool states carry high lock contention during volatility due to multiple high-frequency market makers updating positions in the same slots.
- **Compute Unit Footprint:** **~65,000–90,000 CU**.

### D. Jupiter v6: Dynamic Routing Engine
Jupiter does not hold native liquidity; it builds an in-memory directed acyclic graph (DAG) across every AMM and CLOB on Solana.
- **Routing Algorithms:** Split trades across multiple DEXs (e.g., 60% Orca, 40% Phoenix) to minimize non-linear price impact.
- **Overhead:** Highly complex multi-account serialization; can load 15–30 Solana accounts into a single transaction.
- **Compute Unit Footprint:** **~180,000–380,000 CU**.
- **Best Suited For:** Complex long-tail token routes (e.g., Meme/USDC -> SOL -> Target) and trades exceeding the depth of any individual venue.
