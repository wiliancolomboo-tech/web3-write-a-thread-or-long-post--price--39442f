5/9 Why not just use Jupiter for everything?

Jupiter does split-routing:
E.g., 60% via Orca Whirlpool, 30% via Phoenix CLOB, 10% via Raydium CLMM. 

However, route computation introduces serialization overhead (~40-80ms). For HFT, proprietary arbitrage, or institutional liquidation engines, direct on-chain PDA indexing beats aggregator routing latency every time.
