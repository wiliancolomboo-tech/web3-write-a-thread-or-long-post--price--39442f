1/ The Fallacy of the Spot Price
On Ethereum, gas fees dwarf small execution slippage. On @solana, slot times are ~400ms. Liquidity shifts block-by-block. 

Spot price ignores three crucial variables:
- Dynamic Compute Priority Fees (Micro-lamports paid to validator priority queues)
- CLOB vs CLMM Taker Fee Invariants
- Instantaneous Pyth Confidence Discrepancies ($conf)

If your execution engine doesn't calculate Net Effective Price ($P_{\text{eff}}$), you are bleeding capital.

2/ Anatomy of the Venues:
🔹 @PhoenixTrade: Pure on-chain CLOB. Deterministic match events. Zero LP curve slippage at tick depth. Lowest base taker overhead.
🔹 @Orca_so Whirlpools: Concentrated liquidity. Outstanding capital efficiency for bounded ranges, but larger tick-crossings induce quadratic slippage degradation.
🔹 @JupiterExchange: Dynamic graph routing across multi-hop splits. The gold standard for retail size, but multi-account write-locks increase compute unit consumption.
