Why does venue pricing diverge?

1/ @Orca_so CLMM: Deep concentrated ticks. Ideal for trades <$25k, but traversing tick arrays costs additional CUs.
2/ @PhoenixTrade: Pure CLOB matching in a single account. Zero tick crossing overhead, lowest gas footprint (~22k CUs).
3/ In volatile slots, account write-lock contention creates phantom slippage before your swap lands.
