2/10 Why do CLMMs diverge from Aggregators?
Orca & Raydium store prices as `sqrt_price_x64` (Q64.64 fixed-point):
Price = (sqrt_price / 2^64)^2 * 10^(decA - decB)

Jupiter routes across split hops, factoring compute budgets and account write-locks. 
When slot volatility spikes, cross-venue spread dispersion jumps >35 bps.
